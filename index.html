01 — ABOUT

Where ideas meet impact.

The Economics Club, IISER Bhopal is a platform for curious minds who want to understand how markets, businesses, policies, data, and people shape the world around us.

Our scope spans Economics, Finance, Quantitative Finance, Investment, Consulting, Business Strategy, Data Analytics, and Policy.

Through discussions, workshops, case studies, competitions, simulations, and hands-on experiences, we learn, challenge conventional thinking, and build real-world skills.

02 — ANNUAL ROADMAP

Core initiatives

A year-long sequence of market simulations, mechanism design, strategy conclaves, and research forums.

01 · TRADING DESK

Stock Market Simulation

Live order-book simulations for portfolio construction, risk limits, and market microstructure analysis.

02 · GAME THEORY

Auction Wars

Strategic bidding under incomplete information: capital allocation, collusion risks, and winner’s curse.

03 · CENTRAL BANKING

Fed Simulation

FOMC-style rate-setting under inflation targets, employment mandates, and financial-stability constraints.

04 · SEMINAR

Case Discussions

Biweekly dissections of policy papers, firm strategy, and empirical econometric designs.

05 · FLAGSHIP

Conclave

Industry–academia conference with keynote talks, panel debates, and student research presentations.

06 · NATIONAL

Case Competitions

National case challenges spanning consulting, public economics, and quantitative market strategy.

07 · COMPUTATIONAL

Winter Workshop

Intensive lab on quantitative finance, time-series econometrics, and algorithmic modelling.

08 · MENTORSHIP

Speaker Series

Alumni and guest lectures bridging academia, quant funds, central banks, and industry.

03 — DEPARTMENT

Economics at IISER Bhopal

Established in 2017, the Department promotes cutting-edge research and rigorous training across microeconomics, macroeconomics, game theory, industrial organization, public economics, finance, behavioral and environmental economics.

It offers a four-year BS in Economic Sciences (with an optional BS-MS dual degree year) and a PhD programme.
